Changes

What's new

What changed in the iPhone and Android apps, newest first.

November 2026

  • Stocks by sector. Holdings has a card showing how your shares split across sectors, by value, with the gain or loss in each.
  • Sector colours. Each ticker on Positions and Holdings takes its sector's colour, the same colours as the sector card.

October 2026

  • Price paid and net after premium. Each holding shows what you paid and what it cost you after the premium you've collected on it.
  • Sell shares. Sell a holding from the app. Shares under an open call are never sold.
  • Your holding on the trade sheet. Before you sell a put or call, the sheet shows the shares you hold, your average cost and what your average would be after assignment.
  • Today's move. The Market Regime card shows the day's move, and the queue lists the favoured side first.
  • Trades this year. A card on Performance counts this year's trades.
  • Weekly recap. Calls sold this week that expire later now appear in the recap.
  • Alerts open their pick. Tapping an alert finds its pick on the first try.
In testing

Being tested, not in the app yet

These run on the leader's account and a paper account first. They aren't available to subscribers yet.

  • Exit planner. For shares below what you paid: put free cash into one stock at a time, in 100-share lots, choosing the one that then needs the smallest rise. Then sell everything at break-even plus 2¢ with an order that stays open until it fills, and move the cash to the next stock.
  • Exit mode on paper. A paper account that stops opening new trades while any stock has been held more than two weeks and is 5% or more below break-even, and works its way out of those stocks with the exit planner.
