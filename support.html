Common questions

When do new picks appear?

Each trading day after 09:45 Eastern time, once the market has settled after the open.

A pick says "Over your limit". What does that mean?

The trade needs more cash than your account allows under the program's rules: one position's size cap, your cash, this week's deployment cap, or the 30% cash reserve. You can still take it; the app asks you to confirm first. Switch to Fits my cash to hide those picks.

Does the app trade for me?

No. Nothing is placed until you approve it. In Follow, you get an alert for each of the leader's trades and tap Copy if you want it.

I copied a call but I don't own the stock.

The app places a buy-write: it buys 100 shares per contract and sells the call in one order, so the call is always covered. It shows you the cost and asks before placing it.

See the Tradier guide. E*Trade is coming soon.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On Android: open Subscriptions in Google Play, choose Premium Harvester, then Cancel subscription. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Delete Account on iPhone, or Account › Delete account on Android. Your account and data are deleted immediately. Cancel any subscription in your App Store or Google Play account first. See the privacy policy.
