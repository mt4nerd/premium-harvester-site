Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-01. Applies to the Premium Harvester apps for iPhone and Android and this website.

Premium Harvester collects what it needs to show you picks, trade in the brokerage account you connect, and send you alerts. It doesn't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your sign-in identifier from Apple or Google, and the name and email address they shareTo create your account and keep your data separate from other users'
Your plan and subscription statusTo unlock the features you pay for
Broker account number and access token (tokens are encrypted)To read your balances and holdings and place the orders you approve
Balances, holdings, orders and fills from your brokerTo size picks to your account, show positions and stats, and alert you
What you enter: Follow account size, favorites, trade notesTo provide those features
A push notification token for your deviceTo send the alerts you've turned on
Questions you ask the AI coach, with your cash balance, open-trade tickers and the screen you're onTo answer the question in context

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or your broker login password.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the app, and only the data each one needs:

  • Your broker (Tradier, and E*Trade when available): your token and the orders you approve.
  • Anthropic, and Google (Gemini) as a backup: AI coach questions and the context listed above, to generate answers. No name or email is sent.
  • Apple (Apple Push Notification service) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): your device token and the alert text, to deliver notifications.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in and subscription billing are handled by them under their own privacy policies.

Market data comes from Tradier and Yahoo Finance. Those requests contain ticker symbols, not your personal information. We disclose data if the law requires it.

Follow and the leader

If you follow a leader, you see their trades. Leaders don't see who follows them, and your trades aren't shown to anyone. A leader's public record shows weekly totals only.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your data while your account is open.
  • Disconnect a broker in the app at any time; the stored token is removed.
  • To delete your account and data, contact us through the support page. We delete it within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.
  • Cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Security, children, changes

  • Data travels over HTTPS. Broker tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • Premium Harvester is for adults who can open a brokerage account. It isn't directed at children under 18.
  • If this policy changes, we update the effective date above and tell you in the app for any significant change.
