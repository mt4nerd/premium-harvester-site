Connect E*Trade

E*Trade connections aren't open to all users yet. When they are, you'll sign in to E*Trade and approve Premium Harvester there. You won't need to copy any keys.

How it will work

  1. In the app, open Account and choose Connect E*Trade.
  2. Sign in on E*Trade's own page and approve access.
  3. You return to the app, connected.

E*Trade ends every connection at midnight Eastern time. Each trading morning, the app asks you to reconnect with one tap before you trade.

In the meantime

  • Without a connected broker you still see the full queue and every pick's details; trade them at E*Trade yourself.
  • Or connect a Tradier account. Its free paper account is a good way to try the program.
