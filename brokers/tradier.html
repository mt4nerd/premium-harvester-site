Connect Tradier

Premium Harvester places your trades in your own Tradier account using your personal access token. It takes about five minutes.

You need
A Tradier Brokerage account, and a Trader or Autopilot plan
Practice first
Every Tradier account comes with a free paper account: practice money and 15-minute-delayed quotes
Other brokers
E*Trade is coming soon

1. Get your token and account number

  1. Open a Tradier Brokerage account at tradier.com if you don't have one.
  2. Sign in and go to web.tradier.com/user/api. Tradier shows two tokens there: a production token for your real account and a sandbox token for your paper account.
  3. Copy the token for the account you want to connect.
  4. Note your account number. It looks like 6YA000001 and is shown on web.tradier.com.

Your token lets software trade your account. Paste it only into the Premium Harvester app, and never send it by email or message.

2. Add it in the app

  1. Open the Account tab and find Connect Broker.
  2. Choose Tradier, then the account type: IRA or Taxable.
  3. Enter your Account ID and paste your token into Bearer Token.
  4. For a paper account, turn on Paper account (sandbox) and use your sandbox token.
  5. Save. The account shows as Connected, and your balance and holdings sync.

What happens next

  • The queue marks each pick against your own cash and limits.
  • Approving a pick shows you the live quote and the order before anything is sent.
  • Your holdings sync from Tradier before the open and after the close, and whenever you tap Sync. Order fills update on their own.
  • Your token is stored encrypted on our server and used only to read your account and place the orders you approve. To stop it, tap Disconnect on the account in the app; the stored token is deleted. You can also manage your token on Tradier's API page.
