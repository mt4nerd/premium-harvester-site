What you get on Android

  • Daily Protocol. A short checklist that walks you through the trading morning in order.
  • Today. Market regime, progress toward 1% this week, and anything that needs attention.
  • The queue. Scored covered calls, cash-secured puts and buy-writes. Switch between picks that fit your cash and all of them.
  • Stock detail. Price chart, AI analysis, key dates and the full options chain for any ticker.
  • Positions. Premium captured against the 50%, 70% and 85% marks, with roll and close.
  • Follow (Autopilot). A push alert for each of the leader's trades and one-tap Copy.

Get started

  1. Install the app and sign in with Google.
  2. Choose Trader or Autopilot and start your 14-day free trial.
  3. Connect your broker in Account. See the Tradier guide.
  4. After 09:45 ET on a trading day, open Queue for the morning's picks.
