The strategy

The wheel, run on rules

Premium Harvester runs the wheel: sell puts on stocks you'd be happy to own, and sell calls on the shares you end up with. Each step collects premium.

  1. Sell a cash-secured put

    Collect premium for agreeing to buy 100 shares at the strike.

  2. Get assigned, or keep the premium

    If the stock closes below the strike you own the shares. If not, the premium is yours and you sell another put.

  3. Sell a covered call

    Collect premium on the shares, at a strike above what you paid.

  4. Get called away, and start again

    Your shares sell at the strike. Back to step 1 with the cash.
